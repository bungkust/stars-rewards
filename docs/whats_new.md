# What's New - Star Habit v1.3.7

Release notes for the Google Play Store update.

## English (en-US)
```text
- Google Play Store Rating: Share your feedback with us easily via the new app review prompt in Settings or after verifying missions!
- Customized Notifications: Added fine-grained switches in Settings to customize notifications specifically for daily reports, missed missions, and approvals.
- Timezone Reliability: Improved consistency of daily streaks and task completion records across different timezones.
- Data Backup Upgrades: Exports now include custom parent profile settings and notification preferences for a seamless restore experience.
- Bug Fixes: Corrected balance adjustments during task verifications and reward redemptions.
```
